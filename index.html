How to Use Crazy Chat

Simple, fast messaging directly from your browser—no room codes or downloads required.

Free & Web-Based
01

Sign In with Google

Visit crazy-chat.com and click "Sign in with Google". Your Google profile name, email, and avatar are used automatically so you never have to remember extra passwords.

02

Start a Chat by Email

No room codes needed! Type your friend's Gmail/Google address into the "Start a chat by email" bar at the top of the Chats screen and tap Start.

03

Voice Calls, Voice Notes & Text

Chat in real time with instant text messages, send crisp voice messages, or start live voice calls directly in your browser without extra apps.

04

Ask CrazyChatAI Anything

Need quick answers, ideas, or homework help? Tap CrazyChatAI pinned in your chat list anytime for instant AI responses.

Quick Tips for the Best Experience

  • Allow Microphone Access: When prompted by Safari or Chrome, tap Allow so you can make live voice calls and record voice messages smoothly.
  • Add to Home Screen (iPhone/Android): Open crazy-chat.com in your mobile browser, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to launch Crazy Chat like a native app.
  • Always Use HTTPS: Make sure the address bar starts with https://crazy-chat.com for encrypted connections and browser call permissions.

Privacy Policy

Applies to crazy-chat.com and crazy-chat.info

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Crazy Chat is designed to collect only the minimum information needed to let you sign in and message others:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, Google user ID, and profile picture URL.
  • Chat & Voice Content: Text messages, voice recordings, and timestamps you send to other users or to CrazyChatAI so they can be delivered and displayed in your chat history.
  • Voice Call Signaling & Connection Data: Temporary WebRTC signaling data and network routing information (IP address/ICE candidates) needed to connect live peer-to-peer voice calls.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity securely via Google OAuth / Firebase Authentication.
  • To allow other signed-in users to find, message, and call you using your email address without needing room codes.
  • To connect live voice calls, deliver voice notes and text chats, and generate replies when you interact with CrazyChatAI.
  • To maintain service security, prevent spam/abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Microphone & Live Voice Calls

Crazy Chat only accesses your device's microphone when you start/answer a live voice call or explicitly press the record button to send a voice message. Live voice call audio is streamed in real time via encrypted WebRTC connections and is not recorded or stored on our servers.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or messages to advertisers. Your data is processed solely through trusted infrastructure providers needed to run the app:

  • Google / Firebase: Used for Google Sign-In authentication and secure real-time cloud message/media storage.
  • AI Processing Providers: Prompts you explicitly send inside the CrazyChatAI chat are processed to generate responses.

5. Data Security & Your Choices

All traffic between your browser and Crazy Chat is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. You may sign out at any time using the sign-out icon in the top-right corner of the app, revoke Crazy Chat's access anytime from your Google Account Permissions page, or request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

Privacy Contact For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, reach out via our official repository or support contact at admin@crazy-chat.com.
